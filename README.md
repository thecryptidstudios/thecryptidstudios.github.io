<h3 style="text-align: center;">Da Story</h3>
<p style="text-align: center;">DOORS: HAULAGES is a fan-game of the popular horror game DOORS on ROBLOX. We made this due to our passion for the game and because we wanted to do something with some of the ideas we forged, and also thanks to our Discord server, Spacetime, who gave us teh motivation!</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">It takes place in a side-story of the vanilla game of DOORS, where Starlight, an Architect, found a mysterious presence in their realm...&nbsp;<strong>other, brand new</strong>&nbsp;architects. And one powerful one, too...</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">At the (forceful) request of Starlight, you guide them into and across these new floors and lands, documenting everything you can... after all, you were one of the only tourists who wished to&nbsp;<strong>stay </strong>in these realms&nbsp;after all.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">Da Gameplay</h3>
<p style="text-align: center;">The gameplay has a focus on documentation and exploration much like DOORS. However, while DOORS has a motivation of escape, and trying to reach the end, HAULAGES has an in-depth research, crafting, and expansion motivation; DOORS gives the feeling of wanting to escape, and HAULAGES has you never wanting to escape unless to find more unique, captivating things.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">While the gameplay has similar identity to DOORS, we plan on adding a brand new gimmic to the ENTIRE game:</p>
<h4 style="text-align: center;">Hauling</h4>
<p style="text-align: center;">Starlight will host a vessel you&nbsp;<strong>must</strong> guide through the entire game. Be it an item, a backpack or a... minecart??? You will have to carry them along with you the entire way. Use them to complete puzzles, block entities, or even enhance items, they will be your guide, and you will be theirs.</p>
